# song-standard

**Mood:** Accenture Song FY26 master — bold, clean, minimal. Crafted minimalism with bursts of purple.
**Use case:** any ACN SONG-branded deck that should match the official FY26 template exactly. This is the canonical Song look, derived from `brand/acn-song/DESIGN.md` + the FY26 Song Template.

## Cifre stilistiche
- **Mode**: light is the **default** (white canvas, black text). Dark (`#120A1C`) and full-purple are reserved as accents (section openers, hero stats, closing).
- **Tipografia**: **Graphik** only (Semibold/Medium/Regular). Hierarchy by size, never by weight-within-a-string. No italics, ever.
- **Firma**: the **deconstructed bar** — 5 purple segments, decreasing widths (≈360/280/220/140/80), 3px tall, under title moments (cover, dividers, thank-you).
- **Numeri sezione**: gray two-digit (`01`, `02`) above a large headline.
- **Stat oversize**: huge purple numbers (96–180px), small descriptor below.
- **Forme**: rettilinee, **radius 0** (eccetto cerchi: dot timeline, avatar). **Nessuna ombra** — gerarchia solo per colore/dimensione/spazio.
- **Footer**: "Accenture **Song**" (Song in viola) a sinistra; pagina + copyright a destra.
- **Colori**: regola 45/35/20 (bianco / nero / viola). Secondari (pink/blue/aqua) <5%, solo per dataviz.

## Palette (da DESIGN.md — non sovrascrivere con altro)
- primary `#A100FF` · dark `#7500C0` · darkest `#460073` · light `#C2A3FF` · lightest `#E6DCFF`
- ink `#000000` · dark canvas `#120A1C` · gray `#818180` · medium `#CFCFCF` · light `#F1F1EF` · white `#FFFFFF`

## Quando NON usarlo
- Quando serve un look NON-Accenture (allora: ispirazione o custom).
- Quando il cliente vuole un'identità diversa dal master Song.
