# corporate

**Mood:** sobrio, autorevole, formale
**Use case:** board deck, financial report, enterprise sales, partnership pitch

## Cifre stilistiche
- **Tipografia**: serif per titoli (Source Serif Pro, Lora), sans per il body (Inter). Pesi conservatori 400/500/600.
- **Decorazioni**: gradient molto soft, palette neutra + 1 accent solo, no orbs, no big-num oversize.
- **Motion**: minima (fade brevi, no bounce, no scale).
- **Borders**: 6-10px (leggermente arrotondati).
- **Layout**: split classici, gerarchia formale.

## Palette default
- brand-1: `#0A2540` (navy)
- brand-2: `#1E3A5F`
- brand-3: `#5A6B7B` (slate)
- brand-4: `#0A2540` (dark bg = navy)
- soft-1:  `#F5F7FA`
- accent:  `#C8A24B` (gold accent)

## Quando NON usarlo
- Pitch creativi → `editorial`
- Eventi tech/AI → `dark-tech`
- Consumer/community → `playful`
